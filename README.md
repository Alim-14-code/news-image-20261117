Sum up 17 November's world news in one image: a one-page newspaper front page for Tuesday 17 November 2026. Lead story: Ukraine brings down the Kerch Bridge road span in an overnight drone and missile strike. Also covered: Iran fires drones at a U.S. radar in Bahrain, Xi, Trump and Putin to meet in Shenzhen, tankers struck near the Strait of Hormuz, Japan scrambles jets over suspected Russian aircraft, the Pentagon's ninth straight failed audit, and Britain's sanctions on settlement builders. The image is drawn at build time from real reporting (AP, Reuters, Bloomberg, BBC, Just Security) and the program copies it to /output/entry.png.
