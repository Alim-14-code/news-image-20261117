Sum up 17 November's world news in one image: a one-page newspaper front page for Tuesday 17 November 2026. Lead story: Ukraine brings down the Kerch Bridge road span in an overnight drone and missile strike. Also covered: a fourth tanker mined and a U.S. drone seized in the Strait of Hormuz, Nvidia's ousted chief wanting his job back, the FAA halting the routers tied to the ground stop, the Xi–Trump–Putin summit set for Shenzhen, AI stocks leading Wall Street lower, and the day in numbers (20,000 flights hit, 4 tankers mined, 31% lung-cancer survival). The image is drawn at build time from real reporting (AP, Reuters, Bloomberg, BBC, UN) and the program copies it to /output/entry.png.

Round 3 rebuild: same Kerch lead (the day's top world story), but broader — R2 was almost all war; R3 adds business, U.S. domestic, markets and a health number, with a cleaner dusk illustration and a bottom strip that no longer crowds the footer.

mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
